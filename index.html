About the data

The 500 leads are invented: companies across Australia, the Netherlands, India, the UK, Singapore and New Zealand, each with a job title, a source and a history of activity. Every lead also carries a hidden answer to "would sales take this?", based on how ready they are to buy and how well they fit. The score never sees that answer, the same way your marketing automation never hears the first sales call. Use the lab to get a feel for a scoring change before you try it on real data in HubSpot, Marketo or Dynamics 365.
