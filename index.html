How scoring works

Score = fit + engagement

Fit is who the lead is: role seniority, company size, and whether they're in a target industry (financial services, healthcare, government, energy & utilities). Engagement is what they did, like requesting a demo, viewing pricing or attending a webinar.

Old activity fades

An event loses half its points every half-life (30 days by default). Only the latest three of each event type count, ten for page visits, so clicking forty emails can't carry a lead. Unsubscribing takes points off in full.

When a lead becomes an MQL

Its score reaches the threshold and it has done something in the last 90 days. A perfect-fit account that has gone quiet stays in nurture.

Where SQLs come from

Each sample lead has a hidden flag for whether sales would accept it, based on real buying intent and fit. The score can only guess intent from behaviour, so the MQL → SQL rate shows how well your weights read it.
