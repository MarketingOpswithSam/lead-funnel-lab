Sales time is precious

Reps spend only about 30% of their week selling (Salesforce). Scoring decides who gets that time.

2

Most leads won't buy

Fewer than 1 in 100 leads become customers (Forrester). Don't send them all to sales.

3

Who they are vs what they do

The right job title says they could buy. Recent activity says they're buying now.

4

Fresh beats old

A pricing visit last week beats a download last year. Email opens don't count here (Apple, 2021).

5

The line is a trade

Move it right: happier sales, more missed buyers. Left: the opposite.

6

AI learns the scoring

Predictive scoring learns from your won and lost deals instead of rules you set (Microsoft Learn).
